HeartRisk

Heart Disease Risk Predictor — logistic regression, trained on 900 synthetic patient records. All prediction happens in your browser; no data leaves this page.

For learning only — not medical advice. A real check-up needs a doctor, an ECG and lab tests. This demo shows how a simple ML model turns health readings into a risk score.

Enter the readings and press Predict heart-disease risk.

Model: 5-fold cross-validated accuracy = 75.1%, AUC = 0.83 (guessing the majority class: 50.3% — about 1.5× better). Risk above 50% is flagged as high risk. Strongest drivers: ST depression, low max heart rate and high cholesterol — matching known cardiac risk factors. Trained on synthetic data for learning purposes.
